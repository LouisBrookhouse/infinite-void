# Same hand

## Intent
Louis asked for one art pass on 1.4.2, then a second pass: THE MAW darker and fleshy, mines small and metallic, and Big Core plus Xeno Biomech back on their pre-1.2.3 mechanics. Keep the wave-125 health cap. Idle play on his save must still kill both bosses before the 35s escape around waves 120-125. Not a roadmap item. R0X stays next. This branch is merged by hand, without the daily-update label.

## Scope
In scope: the Maw and mine paint, the classic shield and plate behaviour, and the latescale assertions that locked the 1.2.3 layout. Out of scope: hitboxes, the health cap, other test assertions, and a pull request.

## Changes
THE MAW wall uses a 4-tone flesh ramp (`#0b0f1e`, `#1b2b5a`, `#5a2a7a`, `#6b5a4a`) with the planet Bayer and upper-left light. Tentacles use that ramp and stay on the dark tones except a highlight on the lit edge. Eyes and the mouth stay the bright points. No yellow on the creature. Mines keep the 15×13 hitbox and draw a small navy-outlined metal orb with a gray cap. The blink in `draw` is the red core.

Big Core setup is the `3cde506` stack: three walls at `ox = 0`, 14×4, `hp * 0.1`, no `down`. Xeno plates are five invulnerable 6×6 blocks with the old sway (`sin * (3 + i * 2.6)`), no `maxHp`. `aimPoint` no longer searches a gap or a covering plate. `findHit` no longer skips a cracked plate. `applyHit` tinked on every block and does not start a regrow timer. `updateBoss` no longer grows walls or plates back. `HP_CAP_WAVE` 125, bend 100, `HP_LATE_MULT` 3.5, and `DMG_LATE` 1.12 are unchanged. Boss HP numbers were not retuned: both kills landed inside 35s.

`tests/run.js` latescale, the lines that changed:

- Removed `back()` (the old lines 641-649) and the `wall` / `plate` fields it returned (old line 655). That helper only existed to assert regrow.
- Replaced the old lines 699-703 (weak-column aim, `covered === 0`, `covered >= 1`, and `hp === max` / `down === 0` after 0.1s) with four outcome asserts: idle Big Core wave 120, idle Xeno wave 125, and the two isolated wave-125 fights, each `kill` and `t < 35`.

After: min5 wave 20 / 11.133 / 1.052s, min15 wave 34 / 17.9 / 1.581s, min30 wave 44 / 14.433 / 2.961s, idle best 10, warp +13. Still inside the guardrails.

Classic kill times against the 35s escape, Louis save, three identical runs: live Big Core wave 120 in 10s, live Xeno Biomech wave 125 in 34.8s, isolated wave 125 Big Core in 10.67s and Xeno in 17.33s.
