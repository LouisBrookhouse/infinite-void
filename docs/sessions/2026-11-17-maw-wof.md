# Maw wall of flesh

## Intent
The ship only aimed at the armored core, so an idle kit could not damage the tentacles. Rebuild the boss as a wall of flesh with four tethered Hungry, two eyes and a mouth, without changing the phase HP budget or early-wave balance.

## Scope
In scope: aim selection in `src/game.js`, the Maw body and phases in `src/content/bosses.js`, the debug kit multiplier, changelog 1.1.2, DESIGN, STATUS, ROADMAP, this note.
Out of scope: new roadmap content, guardrail edits, and opening the pull request.

## Changes
- While the core armor is up, guns, homing missiles and drones aim at the nearest low-health live tentacle. Phase 3 aims at the living eyes. After the tentacles are gone, shots aim at the mouth. Each tentacle segment's hitbox covers that whole segment.
- The body is a full-width wall of meat across the top of the playfield. Four Hungry hang from it. Phase 3 is the two eyes and their lasers. Phase 4 is the same four Hungry, faster, with the wall lower. Phase 5 opens the mouth. A severed Hungry leaves a stump and the broken length falls in one piece.
- Phase health stays 22, 24, 23, 25.6, then 17.6 plus a 16× mouth. `?kit=1` uses a higher `debugMul` (`eHP * 24/25` over primary DPS) so that idle kit finishes each phase inside the 90 second clock. Real runs and the balance sim still use `debugMul` 1.

## Validation
- Headless Chromium, `?final=1&kit=1&mute=1`, zero taps: three fresh runs cleared all 5 phases in 180s, 182s and 184s. No deaths, no phase restarts, Eidolon granted once, warp back to wave 1. Console errors: none. 61 fps.
- Screenshots: `/opt/cursor/artifacts/maw-wof-phase-1.png`, `maw-wof-phase-3.png`, `maw-wof-phase-5.png`, `maw-wof-sever.png`.
- `npm test`: passed. Docs, 8 unit tests, smoke, save, migration, gating and balance. Balance is unchanged: min5 wave 19 (11.8 on screen, TTK 0.853s), min15 wave 32 (17, 1.588s), min30 wave 44 (14.733, 3.338s), warp +13 DM, idle best wave 10.
